PRISMA-PI LITE FACTORY AUDIO V1.1

Base: factory-audio-v1 @ 299eebcce795dd682ae84b2f55918ba8f4ef6cb3
Scope: restore physical ES8311 speaker playback while preserving unified /admin AUDIO and ES7210 microphone capture.

Diagnosis:
- ES8311 output uses I2S0 STD on GPIO8/9/10/12.
- ES7210 microphone uses I2S0 TDM on the same clock GPIO8/9/10 and GPIO11 DIN.
- Microphone is initialized after audio output.
- Factory V1 web/mic works but physical TEST TONE / WAV output is silent.

V1.1 strategy:
- Explicitly pause/disable the ES7210 RX channel while ES8311 playback owns the shared I2S clock domain.
- Resume ES7210 RX after tone/WAV playback completes.
- Do not change web UI, FILES+, recording format, codec pins, or storage.
